This article is about the Sift as it appears in Minecraft. For its appearance in Minecraft Dungeons II, see Dungeons II:The Sift.
This article describes a planned feature.[1][2]
It has not appeared in any development versions yet, but is planned to be included in a future update.

The Sift is an upcoming dimension first seen in Minecraft Dungeons II that is going to release in 2027. Associated features include a new mob called the blub, alongside several new blocks for which no names have been officially confirmed for release.

Blocks

[edit | edit source]

Several salmon-colored blocks are referred to as orange in color in the Audio Description.[3]

Sift stone

  • A dark grayish stone-like block that has a turquoise tint.

Sift dirt

  • A dark pink dirt-like block that generates naturally below grass blocks.

Darker grass block

  • This version has a dark salmon color but is referred to as orange in the Audio Description.[3]

Lighter grass block

  • This version has a lighter salmon color but is referred to as orange in the Audio Description.[3]

Green grass block

  • A damp green grass-like block visible in the distance in the reveal footage.

Darker short grass

  • This version has a dark salmon color but is referred to as orange in the Audio Description.[3]

Lighter short grass

  • This version has a lighter salmon color but is referred to as orange in the Audio Description.[3]

Green short grass

  • Short green foliage visible in the distance in the reveal footage.

Darker Tall grass

  • It is not clear if multiple shades of this exist from the reveal footage.
  • The closest of these to the camera have their bottom halves mostly obscured by shorter variants; however, it seems as though they are indeed two blocks high, rather than one block high growing on an obscured grass block.

Green tall grass

  • Tall green foliage visible in the distance of the reveal footage.

White tree log

  • A gray tree trunk block, comparable in color to the pale oak log.

White tree leaves

  • Comprises the upper canopies of a generated tree feature in an unnamed, distant forested biome depicted in the reveal.

Weeping white foliage

  • This stalactite-like block, much like pale hanging moss from pale oak trees or weeping vines from huge crimson fungi, appears to hang down from the white tree features, attached to the underside of white tree leaves blocks.

Mobs

[edit | edit source]

Blub

  • A small, passive rabbit-like[4] mob that is native to the Sift and can squeak.

Environment

[edit | edit source]

Biomes

[edit | edit source]

Pink meadow

  • A biome that is floored with salmon/pink dirt-like blocks and grasses.
  • The blub is seen in this biome.

Green Sift plains

  • A biome that is floored with green dirt-like blocks and grasses visible in the distance to the right of the White forest in the reveal footage.

White forest

  • A biome wooded with white trees.
  • Seems to generate trees densely packed together similarly to dark forests.

Features

[edit | edit source]

White tree

  • Generated feature composed of dark gray log-like blocks similar to pale oak logs and white leaf-like blocks.
  • Appears to generate with a thick trunk that thins further up the tree, with the thicker part of the trunk appearing much thicker than the trunks of previously existing tree types.

History

[edit | edit source]

Announcement

[edit | edit source]
See also: Dungeons II:The Sift § History
Pre-announcement
July 10, 2012 Nathan Adams (Dinnerbone) states that there exist no current plans for a new dimension to be added until the Nether and the End, the latter of which was in the game for less than a year at that time, receive further content depth.
October 19, 2012 Dinnerbone states once again that a fourth dimension would not be added until the existing dimensions were further developed.
April 17, 2014 Dinnerbone states that the team would like to implement new dimensions into the game on the basis that they're "fun to add", but that one presumably would not be added "any time soon".
September 29, 2018 ​[more information needed] When prompted on whether another dimension would be implemented into the base game, Cory Scheviak (Cojomax99), Dinnerbone, Jason Major, Anita Surajit and Adrian Östergård state that they are still unsatisfied by the state of current dimensions' content depth, and would prefer to focus on further expansion before implementing a fourth.
November 22, 2022 Brandon Pearce (kingbdogz) discusses future implementation of dimensions, stating that a new dimension would have to functionally distinguish itself from existing dimensions from a gameplay perspective, and also positing them as a solution for spreading environmental content as to not create an overwhelming experience in a single dimension.
August 19, 2026 When asked about why the Sift was being introduced to Dungeons II and not Minecraft itself, Jens Bergensten (jeb_) elaborates on the fact that each dimension needs a functional "reason to exist", which is ultimately noted as a dauntingly open-ended requirement.
Announcement
September 26, 2026 The Sift was announced for Minecraft at Minecraft Live – September 2026.
The audio description of the video identifies the blocks as orange rather than pink.[3]

Trivia

[edit | edit source]
  • Despite Agnes Larsson stating that the Sift is the first new dimension to be implemented into the base game in 14 years, its 2027-scheduled implementation actually increases that count to at least 15, as the End was implemented first in November 2011.
    • As of currently, the End was first accessible in a development version 15 years and 5 days ago (Java Edition Beta 1.9 Prerelease 4, released 2011-10-13), and in a full release 14 years and 334 days ago (Java Edition 1.0.0, released 2011-11-18).
