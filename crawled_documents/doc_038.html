26.52, also known as 1.26.52, is a hotfix to Bedrock Edition released on September 25, 2026, for Windows, Xbox, PlayStation, ChromeOS, and Android, on September 28, 2026, for iOS, iPadOS, and Fire OS, and on September 29, 2026, for Nintendo Switch, which fixes bugs.[1]

Fixes

[edit | edit source]

8 issues fixed

  • MCPE-241108 – Graphical problems appear if Vibrant Visuals Display Brightness is not set to 100% and Anti-Aliasing is set to 1.
  • MCPE-241970 – Cushions disappearing when reloaded.
  • MCPE-242446 – Some projectiles incorrectly collide with players in Spectator Mode.
  • MCPE-242503 – Minecraft crashing and failing to open on iOS after updating to 26.50/26.51.
  • MCPE-242586 – Worlds without achievements.

Other

  • Improved stability for Nintendo Switch devices connected to a network.
  • Corrected detection of cubemap array texture support on older Windows graphics hardware.
  • First-use onboarding now starts the selected multiplayer world while the player's Character Creator appearance is still loading.
