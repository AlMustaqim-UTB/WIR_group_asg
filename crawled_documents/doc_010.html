Wilderness Bound is a game drop that released on September 15, 2026. It focuses on the dappled forest biome, adding poplar trees with three different colors of leaves, a new wood type, and new natural blocks. It also adds stairs and slabs for concrete and wool, cushions, straw beds, the abandoned camp, and improved maps. It was announced at Minecraft Live – May 2026, with the name revealed during MC Championship: Fall Stars.[2]

Experimental features for the update were released prior to the finalized release as part of Bedrock Edition 26.40.

Additions

[edit | edit source]

Blocks

[edit | edit source]

Concrete stairs and slabs

  • Can be crafted with concrete.
    • Can also be obtained by cutting in a stonecutter.
  • Comes in all 16 colors of concrete.

Poplar family of blocks:

  • Poplar sapling
    • Grows into a poplar tree.
  • Poplar leaves
    • Comes in red, orange, and yellow varieties.
    • Produces falling leaf particles that match the leaf color, falling as frequently as pale oak or cherry leaf particles.
    • Emits ambient sounds when attached to any log and next to at least three of the same type of leaves in its four horizontal directions.
  • Poplar log and stripped poplar log
  • Poplar wood and stripped poplar wood
  • Poplar planks
    • Poplar stairs
    • Poplar slab
    • Poplar sign
    • Poplar hanging sign
    • Poplar button
    • Poplar pressure plate
    • Poplar door
    • Poplar fence
    • Poplar fence gate
    • Poplar trapdoor
    • Poplar shelf

Red shrub

  • A red bush variant found in dappled forests that is not affected by biome tint.

Shelf mushroom

  • A mushroom variant found naturally on poplar and fallen poplar trees.
  • Comes in two sizes.
    • Using bone meal on a small shelf mushroom grows it into a large one.
    • Breaking a large shelf mushroom drops two shelf mushrooms.
  • Can only be placed on full sides of blocks.
  • Is bouncy when fallen on by entities.
  • Can be used as an ingredient for mushroom stew, suspicious stew and rabbit stew.

Straw bed

  • A new type of bed which can be used to sleep through the night without changing the spawn point.
  • 4 straw beds are crafted with 3 hay bales.
  • Can be stacked to 16.
  • It can only be used once and is destroyed after use, even when leaving the bed during the night.
  • It breaks when used in the Nether or the End, but does not explode like regular beds.
  • Generates in abandoned camps.

Wool stairs and slabs

  • Can be crafted with wool.
  • Comes in all 16 colors of wool.

Items

[edit | edit source]

New explorer maps

  • Added abandoned camp maps that generate in the common chests of abandoned camps. There are 8 variants that lead to different biomes:
    • Bamboo Jungle
    • Cherry Grove
    • Birch Forest
    • Dappled Forest
    • Flower Forest
    • Pale Garden
    • Swamp
    • Windswept Forest
  • Added new structure maps which generate in the abandoned camp's secret chest loot:
    • Buried ancient city map
    • Buried mineshaft map
    • Desert pyramid map
    • Warm ocean ruins map

Non-mob entities

[edit | edit source]

Cushion

  • A decorative block-like entity that can be sat on and can be placed on any surface no matter the height.
    • Breaks when moved by pistons or when the supporting block is removed.
    • Can be collided by non-solid blocks or blocks that don't cause mob suffocation.
  • Can be stacked to 16.
  • Crafted from 3 wool slabs of the respective color.
  • Comes in all 16 colors of wool.
  • Generates in abandoned camps.

Poplar boat

  • A boat that is crafted using poplar planks.
  • Poplar boat with chest

World generation

[edit | edit source]

Biomes

[edit | edit source]

Dappled forest

  • An autumn-themed forest biome which can be found near cold biomes; it consists of poplar trees, patches of coarse dirt, red shrubs, leaf litter, brown mushrooms, shelf mushrooms, and sparse spruce trees.
    • Fallen poplar trees generate more frequently than other fallen trees.
  • Rabbits, foxes, and the cold variants of farm animals spawn here.

Features

[edit | edit source]

Poplar tree

  • A tree that generates in dappled forests.
  • Can have shelf mushrooms growing on it.
  • Comes in three different colors of leaves: orange, red, and yellow. The color of the tree is randomly decided upon world generation.
  • Fallen poplar trees contain brown and shelf mushrooms as well.

Structures

[edit | edit source]

Abandoned camp

  • A structure that generates on the surface of the Overworld in the following biomes:
    • Meadow
    • Cherry Grove
    • Forest
    • Birch Forest
    • Old Growth Birch Forest
    • Flower Forest
    • Windswept Forest
    • Dappled Forest
    • Taiga
    • Snowy Taiga
    • Old Growth Spruce Taiga
    • Old Growth Pine Taiga
    • Sparse Jungle
    • Bamboo Jungle
    • Swamp
    • Savanna
    • Wooded Badlands
    • Pale Garden
  • The tent consists mainly of white wool stairs, straw beds, and fences that depend on the biome.
  • Its structure varies by biome and may include a stone shelter, a pond, a crop farm, a tree, or overgrown elements like leaves and cobwebs.
  • Contains loot in chests, barrels or a hidden oxidized copper chest.

Changes

[edit | edit source]

Blocks

[edit | edit source]

Hay bale

  • Changed the texture. Mainly the strap part, which has been moved down by one pixel in order to better align with the straw bed.

Spruce leaves

  • No longer produce falling leaf particles in Java Edition.

Items

[edit | edit source]

Map and explorer map

  • Changed textures.
  • The player icon on locator and explorer maps has been changed to always show the direction the player is facing.
  • Explorer maps are now unique items, with their own distinct textures and some being renamed:
    • Buried treasure map
    • Jungle pyramid map
    • Ocean monument map
    • Swamp hut map
    • Buried trial chambers map
    • Woodland mansion map
    • Village map
      • Changed woodland mansion map icon.
  • Explorer maps can no longer be zoomed out using a cartography table.

Mushroom stew and suspicious stew

  • Can now be crafted with any combination of two mushrooms instead of specifically requiring one red mushroom and one brown mushroom.
