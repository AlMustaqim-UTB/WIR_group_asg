Minecraft Dungeons II, referred to internally as Spicewood,[5] is an action RPG dungeon crawler developed by Mojang Studios and Double Eleven, and published by Xbox Game Studios on Windows and Xbox, and Mojang Studios on other platforms. It is the direct sequel to Minecraft Dungeons, and was released on September 29, 2026 for Windows, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and Steam on Windows.

History

[edit | edit source]

Dungeons II was known as "Spicewood" during development.​[more information needed] It was announced officially during Minecraft LIVE – March 2026, with the release date of September 29 being revealed during the June 2026 Xbox Games Showcase.[6]

Media

[edit | edit source]

Minecraft Dungeons II: The Rift

[edit | edit source]
Main article: Minecraft Dungeons II: The Rift

Minecraft Dungeons II: The Rift is a novel based on Minecraft Dungeons II. It is authored by Caleb Zane Huett, who had previously authored the Minecraft: Ironsword Academy series, and was released by Random House Worlds on September 1, 2026.[8]

ARG

[edit | edit source]
Main article: Dungeons II:ARG

An alternate reality game was started to promote the release of the game. It began on March 21, 2026 alongside the release of the Minecraft Dungeons II – Announce Trailer during Minecraft LIVE – March 2026, and ended on September 18, 2026 alongside a video titled "The Conclusion"

Trivia

[edit | edit source]
  • There is a "(very) tiny Halo reference" that Marc Watson put in text that's "not on something players would normally have access to."[9] He also snuck "in some very, very minor references to other Minecraft games, including Legends. Somewhere deep in the game."[10]
  • Mojang had an internal launch party on September 23, 2026, where they played the 12 minute demos from gamescom 2026. The winners were "4 designers with 7:54 remaining."[11]
  • Starting on September 24th, 2026, the title screen panorama on Bedrock Edition was changed to an ancient city, promoting the release of the game.
