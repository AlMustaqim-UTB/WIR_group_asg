The fourth drop of 2026 is an upcoming game drop that will release as Java Edition 26.4 and Bedrock Edition 26.70 in the fourth quarter of 2026. It introduces ice caves, a new cave biome featuring icicles, ice crystals, and brittle ice, as well as the frostbite, ice balls, the Freezing effect and updated jukeboxes. It was announced at Minecraft LIVE – September 2026.

Experimental features for the update will be released prior to the finalized release as part of Bedrock Edition 26.60.

Additions

Blocks

Brittle ice

  • A fragile ice block that generates naturally in ice caves.
  • Does not melt.
  • Can be created when water touches blue ice.
  • Breaks when standing for 4 seconds or jumping on them.
  • Can only be obtained by Silk Touch.

Ice crystal

  • A crystal block which generates on the ground in ice caves.
  • Emits a light level of 4.
  • Can be placed on all full sides of a block.


Icicle

  • A spike block that generates as stalactites on the ceiling in ice caves.
    • Can also be placed on the ground as stalagmites.
  • The column part breaks at a light level of 5 or above or in the Nether.
    • Icicle stalactites falls and deals damage depending on the height.
  • Falling to a bottom icicle will increase damage.
  • Like other spikes blocks, icicles can be broken by a thrown trident.
  • Icicle stalactites will grow longer if packed ice is above them, unless they are in the Nether.
  • It has been confirmed that player-placed icicles will not be affected by light, although this feature has not been added yet.

Items

Frostbite spawn egg

  • Used to spawn a frostbite.

Ice ball

  • A throwable projectile that deals damage.
  • It does more damage based on its projectile speed, having a sharp deceleration like snowballs.
  • Can be used to brew potions of Freezing.
  • Obtained by frostbites, which have a chance to drop them when killed.
  • Deals consistent knockback.

Potion of Freezing

  • Brewed by using ice ball.
  • Gives Freezing effect.

Arrow of Freezing

  • A tipped arrow which causes the Freezing effect.

Effects

Freezing

  • An effect version of powder snow freezing that causes an entity to freeze, dealing damage over time and slowing their movement.
  • Since it had the same effect as powder snow freezing, it can be prevented when an entity wears a piece of leather armor, but will not keep the effect from being applied and will not remove the effect.
    • Frostbites are completely immune to Freezing, while snow golems, polar bears, and strays can be inflicted with it but are unaffected.
    • Mobs that are vulnerable to Freezing (blazes, magma cubes, and striders) have a five times multiplier to the damage taken.

Mobs

Frostbite

  • A zombie variant that can be found in snowy biomes.
    • Zombies convert into frostbites when they stay in powder snow too long.
  • Immune to Freezing.
    • Does not sink in powder snow nor make brittle ice crack.
  • Burns in sunlight.
  • It has two modes of attacking: a melee attack and a ranged attack.
    • Most frostbites use a standard melee attack, which gives the Freezing effect.
    • Some frostbites spawn holding ice balls, which they throw as a ranged attack.
  • Drops rotten flesh and ice balls when killed, regardless if holding one or not.
  • Converts into a zombie when underwater for 30 seconds.

World generation

Biomes

Ice caves

  • A cave biome that generates underneath frozen, cold, or semi-cold surface biomes.
  • On very rare occasions, they generate on the surface.
  • Calcite, packed ice, icicles, ice crystals and sometimes patches of snow can generate here.
    • Giant speleothems made of packed ice can generate here.
    • Brittle ice can generate here, forming deep crevasses with bodies of water.
  • Does not generate glow lichen.
  • Higher chances of ores generating inside blobs of stone and deepslate.
  • Common cave monsters along with strays and frostbites can spawn here.

Features

Crevasse

  • Cracks that are covered with brittle ice and are filled with water.

Sounds

Ice and its variants

  • Added unique sounds for ice, packed ice, blue ice, and frosted ice.

Changes

Blocks

Blue ice

  • When water touches blue ice, it creates brittle ice.

Jukebox

  • Changed its texture.
  • Placing a chest, trapped chest, or any variant of copper chest directly above a jukebox causes it to play music discs as a playlist.
    • When there is no disc currently playing in it, the jukebox takes the first available music disc in the above chest and begins playing it.
    • When the disc is finished playing, the jukebox swaps it with the next available disc in the chest and begins playing that one.
    • Discs are taken and played in slot order, skipping empty slots and other non-music-disc items and looping back to the start when it reaches the last music disc in the chest.
    • Ejecting the current disc from the jukebox while it is still playing immediately plays the next available disc.
    • If the chest has no music discs, the disc in the jukebox plays again.
    • The tooltip of the next music disc includes the text “Playing next”.

Mobs

Drowned, husk, and zombie

  • Now are immune to freezing.
  • Zombie converts into a frostbite when in powder snow for 30 seconds.
  • Drowned and husk converts into a zombie when in powder snow for 30 seconds.
