Tiny Takeover is a game drop released on March 24, 2026[3] as Java Edition 26.1 and Bedrock Edition 26.10. Its theme is "cuteness" and "emotional attachment with mobs".[4] It visually overhauls baby mobs and rabbits, giving them distinct models rather than scaled-down adult versions. The update also makes name tags craftable, and adds the golden dandelion, which when fed to baby mobs, stops them from aging until fed another one.

During development, the update was referred to as Drop 1 of 2026. The final name of the update was revealed on Minecraft.net and Minecraft Monthly on March 3, 2026.[5][6]

Experimental features for the update were released prior to the finalized release as part of Bedrock Edition 26.0.

Additions

[edit | edit source]

Blocks

[edit | edit source]

Golden dandelion

  • A new golden variant of the regular dandelion.
  • Does not generate naturally.
  • Can be crafted using a single dandelion surrounded with eight gold nuggets.
  • Prevents baby mobs from aging when fed, showing downward green particles.
    • When fed again, the mob will continue to age again, showing upward green particles.
    • Cannot be used on baby undead mobs, piglins, and villagers.
  • Can be used to make suspicious stew of the Saturation effect.
  • Cannot be composted or used to attract bees.
  • Attracts piglins.

Sounds

[edit | edit source]

Note block

  • Added the trumpet instrument.
    • It can be heard when a note block is placed on top of any of the following copper blocks:
      • Block of Copper and its waxed variants.
      • Cut Copper and its slab, stair, and waxed variants.
      • Chiseled Copper and its waxed variants.
    • The sound is different based on the oxidation level of the copper blocks.

Mobs

  • Added sound variants for the following animals:
    • Cat: Royal
    • Chicken: Picky
    • Cow: Moody
    • Pig: Mini and Big
    • The mob sounds which existed prior to this update are now part of the Classic sound variant.
  • Added eating sounds for pigs.

Items

Armor

  • Helmets, chestplates, leggings, and boots now have unique textures and models when worn by the baby variants of zombies, husks, drowned, zombie villagers, piglins, and zombified piglins.
    • Does not display trims in that form.
    • Small-type armor stands still use scaled-down adult armor models.

Name tag

  • Can now be crafted using a single nugget of any kind and a paper.

Promotions

[edit | edit source]

Throughout the development of Tiny Takeover, it was described as the "cutest" game drop.[8][9][10]

Downloadable content

[edit | edit source]

On March 24, a skin pack entitled Tiny Takeover: Mob Caretakers was released, consisting of three characters themed after the mobs added in the update. An add-on, Tiny Takeover: Mini Mania, was announced the next day.[11]

Social Media Takeover

[edit | edit source]

Beginning with the announcement of the title in Minecraft Monthly, the update was marketed with the gimmick of baby or otherwise small mobs "taking over" the Minecraft franchise. Within the announcement, there is a running gag of Tiny Agnes and Tiny Vu trying to hack the show and host it instead. On the same day, Minecraft.net was updated with several baby mobs infesting design elements, peeking out of a link to Spring to Life - Official Minecraft Trailer, hovering the Mounts of Mayhem site header, and appearing on the icons for various social media platforms.[12] In the article about the name, Linn Viberg's Mojavatar became covered in chicks, and several previously released promotional images were altered to show chicks interacting with the borders.[5]

This extended even after Minecraft LIVE – March 2026 was announced, with both a trailer and promotional video featuring a takeover of chicks.[13] Within the show itself, the sets were even decorated to be overflowing with baby mobs, giving the appearance of a taken over space. Another trailer was also released, once again centering around babies invading Minecraft. Once the update released, changelogs used images of this same scenario; one in a field full of them,[14] the others seemingly chasing Efe.[15]
