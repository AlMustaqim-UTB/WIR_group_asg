Minecraft LIVE – September 2026 was a Minecraft LIVE event that took place on 2026-09-26T17:00Z. The Deep Dig aftershow featured falsesymmetry at 16:30 UTC.[1]

The show revealed the content of the fourth drop of 2026, as well as revealing the Sift, the newest dimension in over 15 years.

Summary

[edit | edit source]

The stream opens with a reprise of an animation featured at Minecraft LIVE – March 2026, before cutting to several shots of Mojang Studios, including their logo, a cherry sign labeled "pick up after your wolf," a neon sign of their logo, and then several employees talking. A fox appears superimposed over the footage, and after consuming the screen it transitions to none other than Jens Bergensten himself. He introduces himself, and asks for full attention, promising an ambitious show with an exciting conclusion. He jokes about it being an End update, and assures the viewers it's not what he's referring to.

He goes on to announce an initiative, stressing how creative the fandom is and the hurdles it takes to become a professional designer. To help, they will begin an official creator platform. He then segues into a promotion for the release of Minecraft on Nintendo Switch 2, featuring support for Vibrant Visuals. From there he celebrates the tenth anniversary of Minecraft Education, and mentions Minecraft Dungeons II will release that Tuesday, before passing the mic to Agnes Larsson via ghastling.

Wilderness Bound

[edit | edit source]
This section needs expansion.
You can help by editing the section and adding missing information.

Agnes walks down a hall in Mojang headquarters, and excitedly announces they will be announcing some of their current projects on the show. She begins by recapping Wilderness Bound, praising how the community made use of cushions, describing the creativity born through new features as "one of the bestest [sic] things about working on Minecraft." She reminisces about sharing fan creations with developers and other peers, demonstrating several uses of the cushion while explaining it functions as an entity, not a block.

From there she reiterates their long-term plan for the game, saying their priority is "adventuring."

Minecraft Dungeons II

[edit | edit source]

Laura de Llorens Garcia talked about how Minecraft Dungeons II brings a new ecosystem to Minecraft Dungeons universe and a really big loot variety. Then, Måns Olson and Ullis Linder revealed more details about the lore, interconnected world, NPCs villagers, jumping, inventory, talismans, rifts and the Sift.

Minecraft World + Hotel

[edit | edit source]

Minecraft World is the world's first fully themed Minecraft land, created by Mojang Studios and Merlin Entertainments, and will open at Chessington World of Adventures in 2027. It was officially revealed during Minecraft Live - March 2026 as Minecraft's biggest real-world theme park project yet, designed to let guests step into the game and in real life. The headline attraction "Escape The Nether" is a world-first Minecraft roller coaster manufactured by Intamin. The ride will be a multi-dimension indoor coaster, taking riders through multiple Minecraft environments and adventures rather than being a traditional outdoor coaster. The first official Minecraft Hotel was also announced, it will be the worlds first Minecraft hotel, allowing guests to eat, sleep, and play in the world of Minecraft.

Sound Effects Pack

[edit | edit source]

Sound effects pack is a type of resource pack coming to Minecraft soon. Similar to how texture packs apply different textures, sound effects packs will change how the game sounds, allowing for a useful feature for developers further along the line.

Ice Caves

[edit | edit source]

Ice caves are a new cave biome announced that is expected to release in the upcoming Fourth Drop 2026. It will generate with new blocks, such as ice crystals and icicles. A new hostile mob, the frostbite will also be able to spawn there, occasionally holding an ice ball, which can be used for ranged attacks and deals damage relative to its velocity, similarly to an arrow. A new Freezing effect will also be added. Frostbite can give this effect when they attack, or it can be obtained in potion form by brewing an ice ball into an awkward potion.

The Sift

[edit | edit source]

It was announced that the Sift, the dimension from Minecraft Dungeons II, will be coming to Java Edition and Bedrock Edition in 2027, along with blubs.

Agnes says that the Sift is only part of their plans for adventure in the game, but it's not the end of it.

The Deep Dig

[edit | edit source]

The Deep Dig aftershow consisted of Ph1lza and Mojang employees such as Vu playing Dungeons II, giving the first live gameplay demonstration of the new game.[dubious] Following that, Agnes, falsesymmetry and the head of vanilla Minecraft​[more information needed] showcased the new ice caves biome coming soon by doing three challenges.​[more information needed] Then Agnes concluded the stream and an end animation was played.

History

[edit | edit source]
September 13, 2026 The LIVE was announced in the Inbox for all languages except English (United States), saying it will contain "upcoming game drops, a look behind the scenes and more".[3]
September 18, 2026 The message was removed on English (United Kingdom).
September 21, 2026 The LIVE is announced at the end of The Last Stand stream, prompting to "discover the next chapter for Minecraft at Minecraft LIVE".
September 22, 2026 The LIVE page on Minecraft.net is updated for the September 2026 event,[4] saying it will contain "the scoop on game drops, behind-the-scenes secrets, awesome guests, and more exciting stuff to come".
A trailer is posted for the event, saying it will show "a new game drop, looking at Minecraft Dungeons II, and lots of developer interviews and behind the scenes looks".
September 25, 2026 The WeChat livestream description no longer references the exact contents of the live, instead translating to covering "new content coming to Minecraft".
