26.4, the release of the fourth game drop of 2026, is an upcoming game drop for Java Edition, planned to be released in the fourth quarter of 2026. It introduces the ice caves, a cave biome featuring icicles and ice crystals, as well as the frostbite, ice balls, and the Freezing effect, and fixes bugs.

Additions

[edit | edit source]
26.4 block and item additions

Blocks

[edit | edit source]

Brittle ice

  • A new block that can be found in ice caves.
    • Only generating naturally as part of the crevasse.
  • Once a player or mob steps on it, it will start to crack and eventually break.
    • The frostbite mob can walk on brittle ice without breaking it.
  • It is obtainable with Silk Touch.
  • It can also be created by water flowing onto a blue ice block.
    • Any water block that borders the blue ice block horizontally will turn into brittle ice.

Ice crystal

  • A new crystal-like block similar to amethyst clusters, which generates on the ground in ice caves.
  • Provides a small amount of light.
  • Can be placed on all sides of a block.
  • Breaks if the support block is removed.

Icicle

  • A new speleothem-like block similar to pointed dripstone and sulfur spikes.
  • Naturally generates as stalactites on the ceiling in ice caves, but can also be placed on the ground as stalagmites.
  • Can naturally grow up to 5 blocks long when pointing downward.
    • Only grows in areas without nearby light sources and outside the Nether.
  • Icicle blocks melt and break when a strong enough light source is nearby, or in the Nether.
  • Falling icicles deal damage to entities.
  • Falling on an icicle stalagmite multiplies the fall damage.
  • It can be placed in any direction and has a base, middle and tip variant.

Items

[edit | edit source]

Arrow of Freezing

  • A tipped arrow which applies the Freezing effect.

Frostbite spawn egg

  • Used to spawn a frostbite.

Ice ball

  • A throwable projectile that deals damage.
    • It does more damage based on its projectile speed.
  • They deal a small amount of damage and also deal knockback.
    • Deals 4 damage to entities it hits, scaling down with velocity.
  • Breaks on impact with blocks or entities, creating a particle effect and sound.
  • Can be used to brew potions of Freezing.
  • Obtained from frostbites, which have a chance to drop them when killed.

Potion of Freezing

  • Obtained by brewing an awkward potion with an ice ball.
  • Applies the Freezing effect when consumed.

Mobs

[edit | edit source]

Frostbite

  • A zombie variant that can be found in snowy biomes.
    • Zombies convert into frostbites when they stay in powder snow too long.
    • Husks now also convert into zombies when they stay in powder snow too long.
  • Immune to Freezing effect.
    • Does not sink in powder snow.
  • Burns in sunlight.
  • It has two modes of attacking: a melee attack and a ranged attack.
    • Switches between melee and ranged combat based on target distance and whether they have ice balls.
    • Most frostbites use a standard melee attack, which gives the Freezing effect.
    • Some frostbites spawn holding ice balls, which they throw as a ranged attack.
  • Drops ice balls and rotten flesh when killed.
  • Converts into a zombie when underwater for too long.

World generation

[edit | edit source]

Ice caves

  • A cave biome that generates underneath frozen biomes.
  • It consists mainly of packed ice and calcite.
  • Large icicles made of packed ice generate on the ground and hanging from the ceiling.
  • Ice crystals occasionally generate on the ground.
  • Sometimes patches of snow can generate here.
  • Does not generate glow lichen, unlike other cave biomes.
  • Ores generate inside blobs of stone and deepslate.
  • Common cave monsters along with strays and frostbites can spawn here.

Gameplay

[edit | edit source]

Effects

  • Freezing
    • An effect that causes an entity to freeze, dealing damage over time and slowing their movement.
      • The Freezing effect will cause players to shake and eventually deal damage.
      • Causes those affected to slowly become frozen as if in powder snow for its duration.
    • Wearing a piece of leather armor prevents the entity from freezing, just like with powder snow, but will not keep the effect from being applied and will not remove the effect.
    • Mobs will take additional damage from Freezing while they are affected by this effect.
      • Mobs that are vulnerable to Freezing have a five times multiplier to the damage taken.
    • Lingering potion, splash potion, and potion of Freezing can be brewed using ice balls.
    • Arrows of Freezing can be crafted by placing a lingering potion of Freezing in the middle of the crafting table surrounded by eight arrows.

General

[edit | edit source]

Block sound sets

  • Added minecraft:block_sound_set registry containing definitions for sounds produced by different categories of blocks.
    • Format: object with fields:
      • volume – a float between 0.00001 and 10.0, the relative volume at which all sounds will be played.
        • If not present, defaults to 1.0
      • pitch – a float between 0.00001 and 2.0, the relative pitch at which all sounds will be played.
        • If not present, defaults to 1.0
      • break_sound – Sound event, the sound that will be played when the block gets broken.
      • step_sound – Sound event, the sound that will be played when an entity walks on top of the block.
      • place_sound – Sound event, the sound that will be played when the block gets placed.
      • hit_sound – Sound event, the sound that will be played while the block is being destroyed.
      • fall_sound – Sound event, the sound that will be played when an entity falls onto the block.
      • All sound fields are optional.

Environment attributes

  • minecraft:visual/has_sky_occluder
    • Determines whether the sky occluder is enabled for the environment. The sky occluder will occlude the sky box up until a certain angle with fog color with a smooth occlusion border.
      • Value type: boolean
      • Default value: true for the Overworld, false for the Nether and the End.
      • Interpolated: no

Features

  • Added crevasse feature type
    • Carves a winding, tapered crevasse into exposed terrain. The crevasse follows the terrain surface, extends in opposite directions from its placement position and requires a minimum connected footprint before carving.
    • Format: object with fields:
      • max_width – integer (between 2 and 8), maximum cross-section width in blocks.
      • max_length – integer (between 4 and 16), maximum end-to-end length in blocks.
      • min_footprint – integer (between 1 and 32), minimum connected horizontal footprint required before carving.
      • cursor_surface_search_range – positive integer, maximum amount of blocks the cursor will scan downwards to find its next position.
      • column_surface_search_range – positive integer, maximum amount of blocks each carving will scan downwards to find a carvable surface.
      • twistiness – float (between 0.0 and 16.0), amount of horizontal curvature.
      • tapering – float (between 0.0 and 1.0), amount of tapering applied on each step of growing the crevasse.
      • turn_factor – float (between 0.0 and 1.0), intensity of rotation applied on each step of growing the crevasse.
      • carve_depth – int provider, carve depth below the origin terrain surface.
      • fill_depth – int provider, fill depth below the origin terrain surface.
      • surface_block – Block state provider, block placed over the crevasse opening.
      • fill_block – Block state provider, block placed at or below the selected fill depth.
      • carvable_blocks – Block predicate, which blocks the crevasse is allowed to remove.
      • acceptable_barrier – Block predicate, which pre-existing blocks to accept as part of a barrier.

Other Players

  • A new menu which has been added to replace Social Interactions.
  • Only available when playing on a world open to multiplayer.
    • This includes:
      • Playing in singleplayer worlds open to LAN
      • Playing on dedicated servers
      • Playing on Realms
    • Can be opened by pressing the "Other Players" icon button in the pause menu
    • Can also be opened from in-game using the "Other Players" key (P by default)
  • Lists the players currently online in the world you are in
  • Lists players who are not online anymore, but may still be of interest:
    • Players who were just online but left
    • Players who recently sent messages in the chat
  • Clicking on a player's icon in the list opens their Player Options.

  • Added minecraft:freezing.
    • Emitted by entities which are affected by the freezing mob effect.
    • Has no fields.

Player Options

  • Player Options is a new screen accessible through both the Friends list and Other Players.
  • It contains per-player social actions previously spread across the Friends list and Social Interactions.
  • These actions are:
    • Sending or canceling a friend request
    • Accepting or declining a friend request
    • Muting a player
    • Reporting a player
  • Muting a player hides their chat messages until the next time you start the game
  • Blocking players is managed through Other Players.

Network Protocol

  • The server now more consistently disconnects clients when a packet they sent causes an error.
  • Added minecraft:mod_list custom packet payload
    • This packet is intended to inform servers about client mods to simplify debugging.
    • Vanilla client sends an empty packet at the start of configuration phase, as it does not know about any mods – that is still a responsibility of 3rd-party modding platforms.
  • Modified minecraft:intention packet
    • The host field in the serverbound intention packet can now hold additional parameters (previously it stored only the domain name used for connecting to a server).
      • The new format is similar to URI query strings – the domain can now optionally be followed by a ? character and then followed by key=value pairs separated by a & character.
      • Empty values can be omitted (including = sign).
      • Keys and values are escaped according to the standard URI rules ("percent-encoded component").
      • Keys starting with _ are reserved for vanilla use.
      • Users can now input properties in any "Server Address" field in the server list.
        • Additionally, any address in form of <id>@<host> will be parsed as <host>?_id=<id>
      • If a server uses SRV DNS records, the host field will contain both original and resolved domains.
        • If the resolved domain is different from the original domain, the resolved one will be used as the "primary" one, while the original one will be added as _o ("origin") property.
        • Example host string: redirected domain>?_o=<original domain>:<original port>, while the port field in the intention packet will be set to the resolved port value.
      • The field size has been extended to 1024 characters.
      • Note: since the minecraft:intent packet is unencrypted, properties should not be used for any security-sensitive purpose.
  • Modified minecraft:transfer packet
    • Added properties field – a string to string map of properties that will be added to the host field in the minecraft:intent packet sent to the target server.
