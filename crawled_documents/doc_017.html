A frostbite is a variant of the zombie that spawns in cold biomes or when a zombie is submerged in powder snow. Its melee attacks apply the Freezing effect to their target, and it can sometimes spawn holding ice balls, which are thrown as a ranged attack. Additionally, they have slightly more health than the regular zombie.

Conversion

[edit | edit source]

Zombies and husks‌[Bedrock Edition only] transform into frostbites when submerged inside powder snow for 22 seconds and retain equipped tools and armor.

Variants

[edit | edit source]

Baby frostbites

[edit | edit source]

Baby frostbites make up 5% of frostbite spawns. They behave similarly to regular frostbites, with the following differences:

  • They are much faster than normal frostbites, yet they have the same health.
  • The noises they make are higher-pitched than adult frostbite sound effects.
  • Worn armor shrinks to fit their body size.
  • They are able to fit through 1×1 block gaps.
  • They give 12 experience points when killed by the player or a tamed wolf instead of 5.
  • Unlike most other baby mobs, they remain babies indefinitely and never become adult frostbites, therefore golden dandelions do not work.
  • They have a decreased hitbox size.

Jockeys

[edit | edit source]
Main articles: Chicken Jockey and Jockey

In Java Edition, most baby frostbites (except ones spawned from a baby zombie converting when submerged in powder snow) have a chance to spawn as a chicken jockey. In a chicken-free environment, each baby has a 4.75% chance of spawning as a chicken jockey, for a total 0.2375% chance for each spawned frostbite to spawn as a chicken jockey; if chickens are present, the chance increases to 0.4875%.

In Bedrock Edition, 15% of naturally spawned baby frostbites can mount a mob and become a jockey after they spawn. A jockey mob ridden by a baby frostbite adapts to the speed of its rider. A baby frostbite can ride:

  • Adult chickens
  • Cows
  • Unsaddled adult pigs
  • Sheep
  • Mooshrooms
  • Untrusting adult ocelots
  • Untamed cats
  • Untamed wolves
  • Untamed adult horses
  • Untamed adult donkeys
  • Untamed adult mules
  • Adult skeleton horses
  • Untamed adult zombie horses
  • Pandas
  • Adult zombies
  • Adult husks
  • Adult frostbites
  • Adult zombie villagers
  • Adult zombified piglins
  • Spiders that were not spawned as a jockey mount
  • Cave spiders that were not spawned as a jockey mount

Behavior

[edit | edit source]

Attacking

[edit | edit source]

A frostbite primarily uses its melee attack, which will apply the Freezing effect for 3 seconds to their target. Unlike husks, they can apply this effect while holding a weapon.

25%[verify for Bedrock Edition] of frostbites spawn holding ice balls and can throw them as a ranged attack. They throw ice balls at range within 16 blocks while moving toward their target, and switch to melee attacks when they get within 2 blocks of the target. Frostbites also throw ice balls if they pick up an ice ball item off the ground.

Frostbites attack players, villagers, wandering traders, iron golems, snow golems‌[Bedrock Edition only], and baby turtles.

Mob type

[edit | edit source]

Being an undead mob, they are:

  • damaged by the status effect Instant Health and healed by the status effect Instant Damage.
  • unaffected by the status effects Regeneration and Poison.
  • ignored by the wither.
  • affected by the Smite enchantment.
  • a threat to armadillos, causing them to hide in their shell.

Picking up items

[edit | edit source]
Main article: Geared mobs § Picking up gear

Like zombies, some frostbites spawn with the ability to pick up dropped items from the ground in Normal and Hard difficulty, with the chance being higher in the latter. In Java Edition, frostbites pick up items that they happen to walk near. In Bedrock Edition, they actively search for and walk toward dropped items.

In Java Edition, standard frostbites can pick up any item except for glow ink sacs. In Bedrock Edition, they can only pick up ice balls, swords, spears, armor, and carved pumpkins. Chicken jockey riders also cannot pick up any variant of an egg[verify].

If the picked up item is armor, the frostbite equips the armor into its respective armor slot. Carved pumpkins and heads are worn on their heads, and elytra are worn in the their chestplate slot. If the picked up item is a shield, the frostbite equips it to their offhand. It cannot use the shield to block incoming attacks, however.

If a frostbite encounters another similar item, it may drop the previous item in favor of the new one if:

  • Both items are armor/weapons and the new item is better damage-wise (reduces higher damage for armor, or inflicts more damage for weapons) without considering enchantments,
  • Both items are armor/weapons with the same damage reduction/infliction, the new item has NBT tags while the old does not‌[JE only] or the new item is less damaged than the old item

Items dropped by mobs in exchange for another cannot be picked up by players or mobs for 10 game ticks (0.5 seconds, barring lag), but can be picked up by hoppers.

A frostbite equipped with a picked-up item becomes persistent and does not despawn, similar to being named.

Picked-up items do not cause the frostbite to drop more experience when killed.

Powder snow

[edit | edit source]

Frostbites do not sink in or take damage from powder snow, and their jump height and speed is unimpeded by it. They can ascend through multiple layers of powdered snow like scaffolding.

Conversion

[edit | edit source]

Like husks, frostbites transform into zombies when submerged inside water for 30 seconds. If the resulting zombie remains submerged in water, it can subsequently transform into a drowned.

Drowned transform turn into zombies after 30 seconds of standing in powder snow, and subsequently into frostbites if they remain in powder snow for a further 30 seconds.

Jockey

[edit | edit source]
This feature is exclusive to Bedrock Edition.

15% of nearby baby zombies, baby husks, gurgles, or baby frostbites may ride an adult frostbite to form a jockey.

Trivia

[edit | edit source]
  • The frostbite could be considered a successor to the frozen zombie from Minecraft Dungeons, another ice-covered zombie variant which notably also attacks with thrown ice projectiles in addition to the standard melee attacks. Both are very similar in appearance as well, with the only significant difference being that the frostbite uses an original clothing design, whereas the frozen zombie simply reuses the clothes of the normal zombie.
    • The frostbite's texture even includes the palette, positioned identically to the palette in the frozen zombie's texture from Minecraft Dungeons.
