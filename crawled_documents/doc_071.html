The Twisted Cape is a cape that was given out to players as a special bonus by pre-ordering Minecraft Dungeons II before its release.

Appearance

[edit | edit source]

The cape's design depicts the twisted warden's spinal column.

The elytra version features warden bones near the shoulders. From there, it features a sculk texture that leads down to a pair of hook-like curves, resembling the normal warden's head and horns.

Distribution

[edit | edit source]

Players could receive this cape as a special bonus by pre-ordering Minecraft Dungeons II prior to its release, then logging in to the game.[1] Some content creators have received the cape early.[2]

Refunding Dungeons II will revoke this cape. As pre-orders are required to obtain it, purchasing the game again now will not return capes revoked in this manner.[3]
