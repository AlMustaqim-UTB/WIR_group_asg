A log or stem is a naturally occurring block found in trees or huge fungi, primarily used as a building block and to create planks, a versatile crafting ingredient. There are currently ten types of logs: oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, pale oak and poplar; and two types of stems: crimson and warped.

Natural generation

[edit | edit source]

All types of logs generate naturally as part of trees, although fallen trees can only contain oak, spruce, birch, jungle, or poplar. Azalea trees are generated with oak logs. Additionally, one jungle log generates for each jungle bush. Stems generate naturally as part of huge fungi.

In addition to trees:

Oak

Oak logs generate as part of:

  • Plains villages
  • Swamp huts
  • Mineshafts
  • Shipwrecks‌[Java Edition only]
  • Trial chambers
  • Abandoned camps in flower forests and forest
Spruce

Spruce logs generate as part of:

  • Taiga and snowy plains villages
  • Shipwrecks‌[Java Edition only]
Jungle

Jungle logs generate as a part of:

  • Shipwrecks‌[Java Edition only]
  • Abandoned camps in bamboo jungles and sparse jungles
Acacia

Acacia logs generate as a part of:

  • Savanna villages
  • Abandoned camps in savannas
Dark Oak

Dark oak logs generate as part of:

  • Pillager outposts
  • Badlands mineshafts
  • Woodland mansions
  • Ancient cities
  • Shipwrecks‌[Java Edition only]
Mangrove

Mangrove logs generate as part of trial chambers.

Birch

Birch logs generate as part of abandoned camps in birch forests, flower forests, and forests.

Cherry

Cherry logs generate as part of abandoned camps in cherry groves.

Pale Oak

Pale oak logs generate as part of abandoned camps in pale gardens.

Poplar

Poplar logs generate as part of abandoned camps in dappled forests.

Mob loot

[edit | edit source]

In Bedrock Edition, wandering traders have a chance of 25% (increased by 1% per level of Looting) to drop 8 available logs when killed while showing the item as trade offer, if the player holds an emerald.

Fuel

[edit | edit source]

Logs, but not stems, can be used as a fuel in furnaces, smelting 1.5 items per block.

Stripping

[edit | edit source]

Using an axe on a log or stem strips it, turning it into a stripped log or a stripped stem, which act the same as regular logs or stems.

Cocoa beans

[edit | edit source]

Cocoa beans can be placed on the side of jungle logs to grow a new cocoa pod.

Development

[edit | edit source]
May 21, 2009 Notch shows interest in adding wood.
August 25, 2009 Notch shows that breaking wood now drops 3–5 wooden planks.
July 18, 2017 Jeb tweets an image of new textures for iron blocks, obsidian and birch logs.
July 19, 2017 Jeb reappears with an image of all the new log textures.
July 19, 2017 JAPPA tweets an image of a less-blurry birch texture.
July 22, 2017 JAPPA tweets an image of new crafting table, wood planks and wood textures.
September 28, 2019 Crimson and Warped logs were shown as part of crimson forest and warped forest biome.
October 16, 2021 Mangrove logs were shown on Minecraft Live 2021.
September 28, 2024 Pale oak logs were showcased on Minecraft Live 2024.
May 30, 2026 Poplar logs were shown at Minecraft Live – May 2026
