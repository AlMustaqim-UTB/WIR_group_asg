About the game

Minecraft: Story Mode and Minecraft: Story Mode - Season Two are discontinued episodic point-and-click narrative-driven graphic adventure games released in October 2015 and in July 2017 developed by Telltale Games and Mojang Studios based on Minecraft, where players have to navigate through chapters to advance a storyline. The decisions and actions taken by the player can change the narrative.

On May 31, 2019, it was announced that both versions of Minecraft: Story Mode would no longer be supported following the closure of Telltale Games. The servers for Story Mode were discontinued on June 25, 2019.

Featured article

During the events of A Block and a Hard Place, an ultimate weapon is forged to combat the threat of the command block. Created using the command enchanting book, the weapon can take the shape of any other weapon type in the game, up to the player's choice.
